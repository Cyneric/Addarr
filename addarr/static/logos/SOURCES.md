# Application logos

`addarr.svg` is Addarr's own mark: a segmented light ring, graphite base and coral plus symbol. It is also used as the favicon.

Unmodified upstream assets, downloaded on 2026-09-12 and served locally.

- `radarr.png`: [Radarr, Logo/128.png](https://github.com/Radarr/Radarr/blob/develop/Logo/128.png)
- `sonarr.png`: [Sonarr, Logo/128.png](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr/blob/v5-develop/Logo/128.png)
- `lidarr.png`: [Lidarr, Logo/128.png](https://github.com/Lidarr/Lidarr/blob/develop/Logo/128.png)
- `telegram.svg`: `Logo.svg` from the [Telegram logo download](https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots).

The logos identify the services that Addarr connects to. They belong to their respective projects and do not imply endorsement of Addarr.

- `sabnzbd.svg`: [SABnzbd, icons/logo-arrow.svg](https://github.com/sabnzbd/sabnzbd/blob/develop/icons/logo-arrow.svg)
